Salud y Armonía

A full-stack e-commerce web application developed to manage an online store and its daily operations. The platform includes a product catalog, user authentication, online sales, order management, customer management, and an administrative dashboard with sales statistics and reports.

Key Features

  • Product catalog and categories
  • User registration and authentication
  • Online shopping and orders
  • Customer management
  • Product and category management
  • Administrative dashboard
  • Sales history and statistics
  • Order management
